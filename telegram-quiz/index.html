FAQ

FAQ about Telegram quizzes

How do I create a quiz in Telegram?

Open TgDraw, add questions and correct answers, assign points, configure publishing and choose the outcome: no winners, a promo code or random selection.

Which outcome options are available?

You can finish with a standard result and no winners, reveal a promo code or use the built-in randomizer for a random winner selection.

How does the built-in randomizer work?

TgDraw builds a participant pool from your conditions and picks a winner at random. The pool can include everyone who completed the quiz or only those with a required score.

Can I restrict the quiz to subscribers?

Yes. Add the required Telegram channels, and the bot will verify each subscription before the quiz starts.

Can I use a promo code instead of selecting a winner?

Yes. In the selected outcome, participants can see a promo code after completing the quiz without any winner selection.

What results are available after the quiz?

You can review answers and scores and, when export is available, save participant data, question statistics and outcome or winner information.
